# Next up

A pane beside the chat that shows what to do next from your board and what this session has done.

## Board

**Board**:
The threads and notes the pane shows, read from your own data folder.
_Avoid_: dashboard, list

**Thread**:
A piece of work left open by an earlier session, with the next step that would finish it.
_Avoid_: task, session item

**Note**:
A short to-do from your notes, not tied to any session.
_Avoid_: inbox item, memo

## Marks

**Mark**:
Your latest action on one thread or note, with the time you took it. The newest mark for an item is the one that counts.
_Avoid_: tick, status

**Done**:
A mark that says the item is finished. It leaves the board and counts toward what you did today.
_Avoid_: ticked, complete

**Drop**:
A mark that says the item is no longer wanted. It leaves the board and never counts as done.
_Avoid_: delete, dismiss

**Park**:
A mark that hides an item until 8am tomorrow. The pane calls it "Not today".
_Avoid_: snooze, later

**Keep**:
Your word that a thread is still needed, so it counts as to do even when nothing else confirmed it. Keep stays through every later mark.
_Avoid_: still open, later

**Undo**:
Clearing an item's mark so it comes back to the board. The cleared mark keeps its time, so an older mark from elsewhere cannot win back.
_Avoid_: untick, reopen, bring back

**Settling**:
The few seconds after Done when the item stays in place, struck through, with Undo offered, before it leaves the board.
_Avoid_: pending, grace period

**Marks ledger**:
The one place that says how each action changes the marks and what the marks mean for the board.
_Avoid_: mark store, marks service
